Why Donate?

Your contribution supports more than a competition robot. It gives young women the opportunity to solve real engineering problems, take ownership of ambitious projects, learn from setbacks, and inspire other students to pursue STEM.

01

Build & Improve Our Robot

Support the motors, sensors, electronics, and fabrication materials we use to prototype, test, and improve our robot.

02

Compete & Grow

Help cover competition registration, transportation, and essential equipment so our students can put their engineering skills to the test.

03

Outreach & Education

Make robotics demonstrations, coding workshops, and hands-on STEM learning more accessible to younger students in our community.

04

Empower Girls in STEM

Give young women hands-on responsibility for programming, CAD, mechanical design, strategy, teamwork, and leadership.

Building on a Strong Foundation

Obsidian begins a new chapter as Team 27859's all-girls robotics team. We carry forward the previous program's commitment to technical development, competition, mentoring, and community education while creating more opportunities for girls to grow as engineers and leaders.

How Much?

Every donation makes a difference. Whether you help purchase a small component or contribute toward competition expenses, your support gives our students more opportunities to design, learn, and improve.

$10

Helps cover wiring, connectors, fasteners, and other everyday prototyping supplies.

$50

Contributes toward motors, servos, sensors, and essential mechanical components.

$100

Supports larger engineering purchases, design iterations, and outreach materials.

$200

Helps fund robot systems, competition expenses, and STEM education opportunities.

These examples are illustrative; donations support the team's current priorities.
