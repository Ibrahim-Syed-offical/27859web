Why Donate?

Supporting Obsidian means investing in much more than a robot. Every contribution provides opportunities for young women to solve challenging problems, develop technical expertise, build confidence, and discover what they are capable of achieving. Your generosity helps turn ambition into experience and experience into lasting impact.

01

Build & Improve Our Robot

Donations provide the motors, sensors, electronics, materials, and specialized components needed to design, prototype, test, and refine our robot. Each improvement allows our students to apply engineering principles to real technical challenges.

02

Compete & Grow

Your support helps cover competition registration, transportation, equipment, and other essential expenses. Competing gives our students the opportunity to collaborate under pressure, overcome setbacks, and demonstrate their engineering skills alongside other FTC teams.

03

Outreach & Education

Donations support educational workshops, robotics demonstrations, coding lessons, and mentorship opportunities. By sharing knowledge in programming, engineering, and design, we aim to make STEM more accessible and inspire younger students to explore their interests.

04

Empower Girls in STEM

As an all-girls team, Obsidian creates opportunities for young women to take ownership of programming, mechanical design, CAD, fabrication, strategy, and leadership. Your investment helps students develop the skills and confidence to pursue future careers in STEM.

Building on a Legacy of Innovation

Obsidian represents a new chapter for FTC Team 27859. While our team now operates as an all-girls robotics team, we build upon the foundation established by the previous 27859 program, including its commitment to technical excellence, competitive robotics, student mentorship, and community STEM education.

Through previous robotics and STEM initiatives, the program has helped students explore Java, Python, Arduino, CAD, and hands-on engineering. We hope to continue that tradition by creating opportunities for more students, particularly young women, to see themselves as future engineers, innovators, and leaders.

How Much?

Every contribution matters, regardless of its size. Whether you help us purchase a single component or support an entire engineering project, your generosity directly strengthens the opportunities available to our students. Here are a few examples of what your support can make possible.

$10

Helps purchase essential supplies such as wiring, connectors, fasteners, and small prototyping materials used in robot development.

$50

Contributes toward motors, servos, sensors, mechanical components, and other materials needed to improve our robot.

$100

Supports larger engineering purchases, repeated design iterations, competition preparation, and STEM outreach activities.

$200

Makes a substantial contribution toward robot systems, competition expenses, educational resources, and opportunities for our students to grow.

These are illustrative examples. Contributions are used according to the team's current needs.
